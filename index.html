At Emberveil Academy, apprentices learn ancient spells, elemental sorcery, and how to bond with and tame noble dragons high above the clouds.

Why Choose Emberveil

Dragons are part of the school

Our dragons are teachers, classmates and guardians. They live in the Roost, sit in lectures and join every festival.

Guilds across the realms

Graduates become dragon riders, spell wrights, sky wardens and healers in guilds across every kingdom.

Hidden in the clouds

The academy floats on seven cloud isles, joined by bridges of light and dragon-back rides.

Teachers Who Care

Our mentors cradle and guide every new hatchling with patience and warmth, just as the ancient Roost teaches.

Creed, Quest & Virtues

Our Vision

A world where people and dragons live in trust, protecting the realms with wisdom and wonder.

Our Quest

  1. Teach every apprentice the safe and ancient arts of magic.
  2. Raise dragons with kindness, never with chains.
  3. Guard the old spells and discover new ones.
  4. Send out riders and mages who serve the realms.

Virtues

Dragon Taming Programme

At Emberveil, taming means earning trust. Every apprentice passes through four stages with a mentor dragon.

Stage 1

The Egg Watch

Keep an egg warm for 30 nights and learn to hear it hum.

Stage 2

First Trust

Hatchling care, feeding and calm speech. No sudden moves.

Stage 3

Flame & Wing

Fire-safe training, saddle bonding and first flights.

Stage 4

The Bonding Trial

Fly the Ember Run with your dragon and earn the Rider's Mark.
